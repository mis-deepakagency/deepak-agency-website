About Deepak Agency

          
Deepak Agency and Readymades is a family-run wholesale garment business of the Kaswa parivar, supplying retailers, shops and organisations in and around Ahilyanagar since 1995.

          
We buy readymade garments from manufacturers across India, in every category except sarees, and stock them department by department in our 40,000 sq ft showroom on Link Road, Kedgaon.

          
Our motto is सचोटी व नाविन्य: honesty in every bill, and new ideas in how we serve our customers.
